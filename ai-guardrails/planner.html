What the three setups mean

Top-down tiers suit small teams where people cover for each other: a few broad roles with firm guardrails. Role-based modules suit clear jobs: each person gets only the abilities their job needs. Hybrid sits in between: a tier sets how far each person can reach, and abilities set what their job needs.

Read Permissions 101 for the full picture.

You can change anything later

The blueprint only picks a starting point. Every role, ability and protection can be edited in Business Central afterwards, and nothing changes for your users until you go live.
