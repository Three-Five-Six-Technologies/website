Three apps that work inside Business Central

Each one uses BC's own pages, permissions and job queue, so there's nothing new for your team to log into. All three are in development and tested in a sandbox. Early access is open.

Security and permissionsIn development

AI Guardrails

Business Central permissions without the spreadsheet. It suggests a role for each person from what they actually post, checks they can still do their job, and lets you try the new access for a day before it sticks.

  • Roll back any change to exactly how access was before
  • Protect fields like vendor bank details and credit limits on every page, API and Excel edit
  • Auditor-ready reports, access reviews and temporary access that removes itself

TFS EDI

EDI that lives in Business Central and tells you what went wrong in plain words. Purchase orders come in as sales orders; acknowledgments, ship notices and invoices go out when you release, ship and post.

  • Per-partner rules instead of custom code: units, item references, dates, duplicates
  • An exceptions worklist with Fix, Reprocess and Process Anyway
  • Search any value in any file: PO, UPC, store number, control number

Telemetry Monitor

Find out what broke overnight before your users tell you. It reads the telemetry Business Central already sends to Azure and turns it into a health dashboard with a next step for each problem.

  • Errors, lock timeouts, slow code, job queue failures and broken integrations
  • No Power BI license and no KQL
  • About 15 minutes to connect, with a built-in connection test
